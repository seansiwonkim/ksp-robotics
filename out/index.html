A pipe inspection robot

Our first product replaces digging and guesswork. The crawler travels inside live-sized water and sewage lines and records their condition end to end.

Inside the crawler

Camera head
A wide-angle camera with its own lighting records the full pipe wall.
Sensor module
Sonar and laser profiling measure pipe shape and water depth.
Sealed body
A watertight housing keeps the electronics dry in water and sewage.
Control board
Onboard compute tags footage with position as it is captured.
Drive tracks
Tracks grip wet pipe floors and carry the crawler over debris.
Tether
Carries power and data between the crawler and the surface.

How an inspection runs

  1. Deploy

    Lower the crawler through a manhole. It drives the pipe on its own, tethered for power and data.

  2. Capture

    Cameras and sensors record the full pipe wall as it moves.

  3. Analyse

    Our software processes the data and flags defects, ranked by severity.

  4. Report

    Export a condition report your team can use for repair and replacement planning.

Software that reads the pipe for you

  • Data analysis

    Every metre of pipe is logged with position, slope, flow and pipe condition, then searchable by street, asset or date.

  • Image processing

    Footage is stitched into an unrolled view of the pipe wall, so inspectors review one image instead of hours of video.

  • Deterioration detection

    Machine learning flags cracks, corrosion, root intrusion and joint displacement, and ranks each by severity for your repair plan.

About Ksp Solutions

We are a small team of robotics, software and machine learning engineers. We develop the robot and the analysis software together, so the data the robot collects is the data the models need.
